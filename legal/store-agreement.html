Store Agreement

Version 2026-11-01 · Last revised November 1, 2026

This Store Agreement ("Agreement") is between YidAssist LLC (confirm) ("Company," "we," "us") and the business that signs up a store on Day's End Deals ("Store," "you"). The person who ticks the box at store signup confirms they may bind the Store. The shopper Terms of Use, the Privacy Policy and the Refund Policy also apply.

1. Definitions

  1. "Service" means the Day's End Deals app, website, store tools (deal posting, Hype Drop, flyers and Weekly Specials, POS import, QR window sign, Instagram posting) and the people-facing listing of your deals.
  2. "Plan" means the paid monthly subscription the Store chooses on the plan screen (for example Basic or Pro, plus extra locations).
  3. "Store Content" means the deals, prices, photos, videos, flyers, logos, store details and certificates the Store provides.
  4. "Authorized Users" means the Store's owner and the staff it adds in the Team screen.

2. The Service

  1. License. While your Plan is active (or in its free trial) and you follow this Agreement, we let you use the Service to post and promote your deals.
  2. Approval. We may review a new store before its deals appear, and may decline or remove a store at our discretion.
  3. Authorized Users. Staff logins are included. You are responsible for your Authorized Users and for removing access when someone leaves.
  4. Connected accounts. If you connect Instagram, you let us post the deals you choose to that account. You can disconnect at any time. Instagram's own terms apply.
  5. No uptime promise. We aim to keep the Service available, but it is provided as is; we do not promise any uptime, and maintenance or outages may happen.

3. Plan, free trial and automatic renewal

  1. Price. The Plan price, the price per extra location and any taxes are shown on the plan screen and on Stripe's checkout page before you pay.
  2. Free trial. A new store gets a 14-day free trial. Your card is charged for the first month when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then.
  3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. YOUR PLAN RENEWS AUTOMATICALLY EVERY MONTH AT THE THEN-CURRENT PRICE, AND YOUR CARD IS CHARGED AT THE START OF EACH MONTHLY PERIOD, UNTIL YOU CANCEL.
  4. How to cancel. Cancel at any time in the app: My store › Plan › Manage billing › Cancel plan, or by emailing [support email]. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid month; your deals stay up until then and no further charges are made.
  5. Reminders. We (through Stripe) email a reminder before a free trial ends and a receipt for each renewal charge.
  6. Price changes. We will tell you at least 30 days before a price change takes effect; you can cancel before then.
  7. Payment. Payments are processed by Stripe under its terms. If a payment fails, Stripe retries it; if it still fails, we may pause your deals until it is paid.
  8. Refunds. See the Refund Policy (https://zalmydev-max.github.io/legal/refund).
  9. Referral credits. Referral rewards are account credits, not cash, and follow the rules shown in the app.
  10. Taxes. Prices do not include sales or other taxes unless the checkout page says so; you pay any taxes that apply.

4. Your responsibilities

  1. Food safety and the law. You are solely responsible for the food you sell: that it is safe, wholesome and legal to sell; properly stored, handled and labeled (including ingredients and the major food allergens) under federal, New York State and New York City rules; and that you hold every license and permit you need.
  2. Accurate deals. Each deal must be real and accurate: the item, the "was" price (a price you actually charged), the deal price, the quantity, the best-by or sell-by date where relevant, and the end time. Honor the posted price while the deal is live and stock remains. Mark items sold out promptly.
  3. Allergens and best-by dates. Do not post food past a use-by date or that is unsafe. Tell shoppers about allergens when asked and keep labels on the product.
  4. Kashrus. You are responsible for the kashrus of your store and products, together with your certifying agency. Upload a current hechsher certificate and keep it current. We check that the certificate is current and block deals when it expires, but we do not certify kashrus. Do not claim a hechsher you do not have.
  5. Store Content. You own your Store Content and confirm you have the rights to it (including photos and music in videos). You give us a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, show, adapt (for example resize or put into a flyer or banner) and share it to run and promote the Service, including on social media you connect. This license ends when you remove the content or close the store, except for copies already shared.
  6. Shopper data. You get only what the Service shows you (for example counts and pickups). Use it only to serve those shoppers and do not contact them for marketing unless they have agreed.
  7. Restrictions. Do not misuse the Service, post misleading deals, scrape or resell data, or use the Service for anything unlawful.

5. Ownership and data

  1. The Service and its software, designs and trademarks belong to Company or its licensors.
  2. We may use aggregated, de-identified data (for example how many deals sell out) to run and improve the Service.
  3. Personal information is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. Billing records are kept for 7 years for tax.
  4. Feedback. You let us use any suggestions freely.

6. Warranties and disclaimers

  1. Mutual warranties. Each party has the authority to enter into this Agreement.
  2. Your warranty. You warrant that your Store Content is accurate and lawful and that your food meets the law.
  3. Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS STATED ABOVE, THE SERVICE IS PROVIDED "AS IS," WITHOUT WARRANTIES OF ANY KIND, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF SALES, TRAFFIC OR RESULTS.

THESE LIMITS DO NOT APPLY TO YOUR INDEMNITY OBLIGATIONS OR YOUR PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS.

8. Indemnification

You will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Company and its officers, employees and agents from any third-party claim arising from the food you sell (including illness, allergic reactions and labeling), your kashrus claims, your Store Content, your deals and prices, or your breach of this Agreement or the law.

9. Term and termination

  1. Term. This Agreement starts when you sign up and continues while your store has an account.
  2. By you. Cancel your Plan as described in section 3; you may also close your store and delete your account in the app.
  3. By us. We may suspend or close a store for non-payment, unsafe or misleading deals, an expired or false hechsher, or a material breach, with notice where reasonable.
  4. Effect. When the store closes, its deals are hidden. Sections 3.7, 4.5 (copies already shared), 5, 7, 8 and 10 survive.

  1. Governing law. The laws of the State of New York (confirm) govern this Agreement. Disputes go to the state and federal courts in [county], New York.
  2. Notices. We send notices to the store owner's sign-in email. Send notices to [support email] or [address].
  3. Relationship. The parties are independent contractors. We are not your agent, partner or employer.
  4. Publicity. We may show your store's name and logo in the app and in promotion of the Service while you are a customer. You can ask us to stop.
  5. Assignment. You may not transfer this Agreement without our consent; we may assign it to a successor.
  6. Force majeure. Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control (except payment).
  7. Changes. We may update this Agreement with 30 days' notice in the app or by email; continuing after that means you accept it.
  8. Entire agreement. This Agreement, with the documents it names, is the entire agreement about the Service for stores.
