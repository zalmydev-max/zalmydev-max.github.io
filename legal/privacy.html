Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 1, 2026 (version 2026-11-01).

YidAssist LLC (confirm) ("YidAssist (confirm)", "we", "us" or "our") runs Day's End Deals, an app and website where food stores post end-of-day discounts and shoppers find them nearby (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy describes how we process personal information about shoppers, store owners and store staff in connection with the Service.

Short version. We collect what the Service needs: your email to sign in, what you choose for alerts, and, for stores, the store's details and plan. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see full card numbers. We do not sell personal information and show no third-party ads. You can download your data or delete your account in the app at any time (Me › Account).

Personal information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Contact data: your email address (used to sign in with a one-time code). Store owners also give the store's name, address, phone, opening hours and the owner's name.
  • Profile and preference data: your shopping list, kosher filters (for example Cholov Yisroel, Pas Yisroel, Glatt), the area you want alerts for (a point on the map and a radius), quiet hours and time zone, saved stores and deals.
  • Activity data: deals you tap I'm going on, pickups you check in for (I picked this up), and sold-out reports you send.
  • Store data: deals, prices, quantities, photos, videos and flyers a store posts; hechsher certificates and their expiry dates; team members' emails and roles; referral codes; and, if the store connects it, its Instagram account.
  • Billing data (stores): the plan, number of locations, subscription status and invoices. Card details are entered on Stripe's page and kept by Stripe.
  • Agreement records: which version of these documents you accepted, when, and on which screen.
  • Communications data: messages you send us.

Automatic data collection

  • Location data: with your permission, your device's location while you use the app, to show deals near you and to check that a pickup happens at the store (we store the distance from the store at check-in, not your route). If you set an alert area, we store that point and radius.
  • Device data: a push-notification token if you allow notifications, the platform (Android, iOS or web) and basic technical logs (time, error messages, IP address) kept by our hosting providers for security.
  • Crash reports and app events: when crash reporting is switched on in a build, the app sends crash reports (error, device model, OS version, your account ID) to Firebase Crashlytics, and the website sends basic usage events (for example "signed in", "opened billing"; no deal content, no email) to Google Analytics for Firebase.
  • We do not use advertising IDs, cross-app tracking or third-party advertising cookies.

How we use your personal information

  • Service delivery: to sign you in, show deals and stores near you, apply your kosher filters, run Hype Drop (I'm going / I picked this up), show a store's live stock, and let stores post deals, flyers and Weekly Specials.
  • Deal alerts: only if you turn them on. We ask first, and alerts are off until you say yes. You can turn them off in the app at any time.
  • Store accounts and billing: to run the store's subscription, trial and referral credits, and to send billing emails (through Stripe).
  • Safety and trust: to check that a store's hechsher certificate is current, to stop abuse (for example false sold-out reports), and to keep the Service secure.
  • Compliance: to keep records the law requires (for example tax records for store billing) and to enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information, do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and do not use it to train AI models.

Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed, then a daily cleanup deletes it:

  • sign-in codes: one day after they expire;
  • notification send logs and alert history: 30 days;
  • Instagram post logs and payment-webhook logs: 90 days;
  • I'm going taps, pickup check-ins and sold-out reports: 12 months;
  • your account, preferences and agreement records: until you delete your account;
  • store billing records and invoices: 7 years, for tax;
  • a record that a deletion was carried out (with no name or email): kept as proof.

How we share your personal information

With the store. When you tap I'm going or check in a pickup, the store sees the count and the pickup (not your email). Stores see their own team members.

Service providers that run the Service for us, under their terms and data-processing agreements:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage, Crashlytics crash reports, Analytics on the website) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications; on iPhone it passes through Apple's push service);
  • Cloudflare (our server, the website and the media/photo delivery network);
  • Stripe (store subscriptions, card payments, invoices and billing emails);
  • Resend (sends the sign-in code emails);
  • Instagram (Meta): only when a store connects its Instagram account, to post that store's deals to it;
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images; they see the map area and your IP address).

Legal and business transfers. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people or the Service, or to a successor in a merger or sale of the business.

Your choices

  • Download my data: Me › Account › Download my data gives you a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Delete my account: Me › Account › Delete my account, or on the web at https://zalmydev-max.github.io/account/delete. A store owner's plan is set to stop at the end of the paid month and the store is closed; billing records are kept as stated above.
  • Deal alerts: off unless you turn them on; change it any time in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Location: you can turn location off in your phone's settings; nearby deals and pickup check-in will ask again.
  • Email: we send only sign-in codes and, to stores, billing emails. If we ever send marketing email, it will be opt-in with an unsubscribe link.

Other sites and services

Store links, Instagram posts and map links lead to services we do not control; their own privacy policies apply.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards: one-time email codes instead of passwords, encrypted connections (HTTPS), access rules on the database so people see only their own data, and limited staff access. No system is completely secure.

International data transfer

We are based in the United States and our service providers may process data in other countries.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and store accounts are for people 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date and version above and ask you to review them in the app.
